## Service and permitted use

Veriq is an early preview for bounded static checks of exported n8n workflows against human-reviewed rules. Use it only with files you own or have permission to process. ## Meaning of results

Veriq does not execute workflows or verify live integrations, manager identity, authorization, or regulatory compliance. A PASS applies only to the supported static model and the supplied rule assumptions. Unsupported or unresolved behavior requires human review. Draft requirements are not approved rules.
